This page has been changed into a memoriam for December Holiday.

December was the lead singer and lead vocalist of our band. She will forever be missed. Below is some media related to her to keep her punky rockstar spirit alive.

December was featured in multiple songs from our band. Including but not limited to: "Raise Up Your Bat", "Starbase Superstar", "Green and Mean", "Shredding The Stars", etc.

Please do not harass her sister Noelle Holiday or any of the Holiday family. The reason for her death was outside of their control. Please understand that we will attempt to keep the band going. But without her, it will be hard to be the same.
