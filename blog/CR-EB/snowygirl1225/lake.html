So I took her hands, and we just started walking.. I kept saying this phrase I cant remember. And I started to feel the cold water. I paniced, but She just told me we would make it.. And that it would be okay. And.. That was calming. It stopped feeling like a nightmare. It was like She was back to comfort me again.. And I started to feel some sort of will to perservere.. TO keep going. And.. When our heads went underwater, I swear I could see.. Someone on the shore looking back. I couldnt make them out until we were fully submerged. And I jolted awake.

But that feeling.. The thing I felt. It never left.
