Back

So I used to play this game called "Egg Walkerz", it was some game about breeding wierd eggs with legs. And if you bred them, it merged their attributes into different ones. I even made a document about which ones were the rarest to get faha.

Anyways, this game had an online mode where you could make the egg things fight eachother, and whoever won got to take one of the enemies egg things (hence why I didnt play it much XP). But I decided to try it out once, and my enemy was some guy with weirdly op and probably hacked eggs. I knew I was done for, but the games anticheat (which I didnt know it had) kicked in and banned them on the spot! For some reason, it allowed me to pick one of their eggs. So I picked the one that had what I thought at the time was the coolest stats. And that was it, for a while...

Before I say this, I would like to mention that the game had a sorta janky system for handling corrupted eggs. Basically, if a created egg was corrupted or incompatable. It would just freeze at the reveal sequence, show a message that just says "ERR 125 INCOMPATABLE_EGG" with the only option being "ok" in all lowercase. And if you pressed it, the game just crashed and reset back to your last save. Which caused issues with me in the past faha.. So whenever one of my weirder eggs were about to breed, I dragged them away and put them in "timeout corners" because I was paranoid of loosing all my progress again (I didn't know how to manually save, and the game only autosaved when you got a new egg). So well, everything was fine for a while until..
